/ About

Hands-on IT, learned by doing

I am an IT professional based in Windsor, Ontario, with an Advanced Diploma in Computer Systems Networking and Telecommunications from St. Clair College.

Currently working as a Field Service and Sales Support / IT Technician at Advance Business Systems, I support business IT environments through network troubleshooting, server administration, firewall configuration, workstation deployment, remote support, and infrastructure maintenance.

My hands-on experience includes WatchGuard firewalls, Cisco networking equipment, VMware ESXi, Microsoft Windows environments, Synology NAS systems, VPN technologies, and IT service management platforms.

I enjoy solving complex technical problems, improving existing infrastructure, and exploring technologies that make IT environments more reliable, secure, and efficient. Beyond day-to-day support, I'm particularly interested in infrastructure modernization, virtualization, automation, and building scalable IT solutions.
